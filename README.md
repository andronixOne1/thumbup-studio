# ThumbUp Studio Web 🎨

> **Next-Generation AI Thumbnail Production Suite** running 100% in your browser.  
> No Node.js, no Python, and **no administrator rights required**.

---

## ✨ Features

- **🎯 Stage-by-Stage Production Wizard**:
  1. **Target Dimensions & Aspect Ratios**: YouTube (1920×1080), Shorts/TikTok (1080×1920), Instagram (1080×1080), or custom dimensions.
  2. **Image Sourcing & Search**: Search games or upload custom artwork with multi-version switcher.
  3. **Crop & Rounded Corners**: Squircle curvature smoothing, smart positioning, and generative empty space fill.
  4. **Pro Typography**: Google Fonts auto-loader, local font detection, dual stroke, 3D extruded shadows, and gradient text fills.
  5. **Gradients & Framing**: Multi-stop linear and 2-color radial gradients with focal center controls, inner strokes, and vignettes.
  6. **Interactive Live Review**: 4-grid and fit-to-frame zoom inspector with manual brush inpainting eraser.
  7. **Batch Export**: Instant high-resolution `.zip` packaging and client-side downloads.

---

## ⚡ Client-Side Architecture (Zero Server Cost)

- **Pure Browser Canvas**: All rendering, gradient math, filters, and layer composition execute natively on the client device.
- **In-Browser Inpainting**: Erases text, watermarks, and blemishes using client-side diffusion and texture synthesis.
- **Privacy First**: Your images and designs never leave your browser.
